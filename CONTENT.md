## Case-study template

Every case study answers, in order, skimmable first:

1. **Header card** — project, role, timeframe, one-sentence outcome with the headline metric.
2. **The problem** — 2–3 sentences, plain language, what was actually at stake.
3. **What I did** — the decisions, not the job description. What was chosen *between*.
4. **Outcome** — numbers. Every asset shown pairs with a metric.
5. **What I'd do differently** — one honest paragraph. (This is what reads human.)

## Placeholder-metrics rule (hard rule)

Draft copy may use `{{TK: description of the real number needed — e.g. "merch revenue"}}`
placeholders so structure can be built before Blake supplies real data. **The site never
deploys with a TK in it.** Enforced, not promised: the build fails if `{{TK` appears in
any content file (predeploy grep). No invented metrics, ever — placeholders are loud and
machine-detectable by design.
